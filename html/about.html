ABOUT US

We are Litch.

More than a gaming platform. Litch was created to connect players, discover new experiences and make gaming accessible anywhere.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect players from all over the world, offering a wide variety of games, innovative features and new ways to experience gaming.

We believe games are more than entertainment. They can create stories, friendships and unforgettable experiences.

THE PLATFORM

More than a place to buy games.

Litch was designed as a complete environment for players. Instead of being only a game store, the platform brings together discovery, community, services and new ways to experience games.

Players can explore new titles, discover highlights, interact with the community, learn more about Litch+ and find support whenever they need it.

Our goal is to create an ecosystem where everything related to gaming can be found in one place.
