THE PARTNERSHIP

Different identities.
Shared direction.

The partnership between Litch and VYRA represents the connection between two independent projects. Each keeps its own identity while opening space for collaboration, exchange of ideas and stronger presentation.

On the Litch side, the partnership is part of the platform's broader idea: creating an ecosystem that is not limited to a store, but can also connect projects, creators and communities.
